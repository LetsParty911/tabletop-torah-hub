The live homepage and current-period settings will present Yom Kippur alone. Haazinu publications remain published and available through the archive, but are no longer included in the current homepage collection.

## Changes
- Replace the active combined-week setting with `Yom Kippur` so the homepage uses the existing Yom Kippur collection only.
- Update the current “What’s New” message to mention Yom Kippur only.
- Refine the homepage’s Yom Kippur state to show:
  - `Yom Kippur`
  - `Yom Kippur Divrei Torah & Preparation`
  - `Explore this week's Yom Kippur selections.`
- Leave ordinary parsha weeks, other Yom Tov weeks, archive content, filters, analytics, downloads, and layout unchanged.

## Validation
- Check phone and desktop views for no Shabbos Shuva/Shuvah or Haazinu promotion.
- Confirm the current collection contains only Yom Kippur material and Haazinu remains available in the archive.
- Confirm current banners/callouts are Yom Kippur-appropriate, downloads and filters still work, and no horizontal overflow appears.
